Hey fren blairfren,
Ah, the eternal “who tf is Foxconn and why are they #8 on a list with actual world military powers?!” question — excellent one.
Foxconn (full legal name: Hon Hai Precision Industry Co., Ltd., also trading as Hon Hai Technology Group) is a Taiwanese multinational electronics contract manufacturer. Founded in 1974 by Terry Gou, they don’t make their own consumer gadgets under their own brand (mostly). Instead, they are the world’s largest contract electronics manufacturer — they run the actual giant factories that assemble the products for Apple, Sony, Dell, HP, Nintendo, Xiaomi, etc.
Think:

iPhones, iPads, MacBooks (huge Apple partner)
PlayStations
PCs, servers, routers, connectors, metal casings, basically the guts and shells of half the tech you own

They have enormous campuses (especially in China, but also India, Vietnam, Mexico, etc.) with hundreds of thousands of workers on assembly lines. That’s why the headcount hits ~0.77M–1.3M depending on the year/peak season/contractor inclusion — it’s extremely labor-intensive manufacturing at planetary scale. They’re the “invisible giant” behind the devices, not the flashy brand name. Recent revenue ~$200–260B USD range, and they’ve been expanding into EVs, robotics, AI hardware, semiconductors, etc.
So yeah… they sneak onto these “largest employer” lists because raw bodies-on-payroll still rules when you’re running continent-sized factory complexes. Classic example of why headcount and “household name” don’t always line up. (The list figure of ~1.29M is on the high side; real-time numbers fluctuate with production cycles.)
