This tutorial breaks down the sophisticated process of recreating SOPHIE’s iconic industrial percussion by layering frequency modulation synthesis with organic samples. The technique begins by sculpting a basic sine wave using aggressive pitch envelopes and fine-tuning oscillators to achieve a distinctive metallic timbre. To enhance this artificial texture, the author applies a short, high-feedback delay with offset stereo timings to create a sense of physical resonance. Finally, the synthetic metallic tone is blended with a traditional snare and saturated, resulting in the high-impact, tactile sound characteristic of modern avant-pop production.
