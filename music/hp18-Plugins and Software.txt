Arturia DX7 + Razor by Native Instruments
Cook's basslines often emerge from a blend of Arturia DX7 and Native Instruments Razor, two synths with wildly different personalities. The DX7 provides gritty, nostalgic FM tones with a lo-fi charm, while Razor's additive synthesis introduces sharp harmonics and a modern edge. On tracks like "Apple," the interplay between these synths creates a bassline that oscillates between warmth and clarity, mirroring the emotional tension of the record.
Cook isolates and tweaks each layer to emphasize their quirks, resulting in basslines that feel both intentional and experimental. By pairing the vintage tones of the DX7 with the futuristic sheen of Razor, Cook pushes the boundaries of sound design, proving that bold contrasts often lead to the most striking results.
